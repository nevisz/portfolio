Windsong Opera Festival

Overview

This academic assignment involved creating a cohesive visual identity for a fictional music festival for a particular music genre. Windsong is a hypothetical opera festival held in Taipei, Taiwan, and follows the tales of forbidden lovers who love is cradled, carried and delivered by the wind.

Ideation

The intent was to blend Chinese/Taiwanese aesthetics with contemporary minimalism to create cultural/folk, modern/elegant visuals. To fulfill the cultural/folk visuals, I initially planned to incorporate:

  • National colours (ie. some combination of red, magenta, gold, blue, jade)
  • Recurring nature elements (ie. water ripple/waves, wind lines, the moon)
  • Recurring symbols (ie. the plum blossom and traditional Chinese patterns)

To fulfill the modern/elegant visuals, I initially planned to pair gold with red and/or blue, and pair a script font with a serif or sans-serif font, using thin lines will reinforce this further.

Learning Hurdles
  • Working with a colour palette with more than 3 colours, and mixing up their roles (ie. primary, secondary and accent colours)
  • Having colours within the larger colour palette create colour dissonance depending on how they're used together
Learning Outcomes
  • Interpeting a professional design brief
  • Repurposing brand assets and visuals for different mediums
  • Text hierarchy
  • Script type should be used sparingly as it can harm both legibility and readability. This is especially the case for script type with thin, natural strokes.
