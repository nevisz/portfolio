Ludera Website Redesign

Overview

This outlines the work I did for Ludera during the Riipen April 2026 Cohort, where I was responsible for proposing and implementing a potential website redesign in Figma. Ludera is an edtech, gaming startup dedicated to redefining the landscape of learning.

For the sake of privacy, I will not be disclosing any Ludera-specific images.

Identifying Pain Points/Areas of Improvement
  1. Mixed Brand Messaging
    Ludera was positioned as a "game first," but its website used traditional edtech copy (ie. "study", "notes," "lecture slides", "flashcards"), making it look like another thinly-veiled quiz app with gamification. The desired positioning is to make Ludera to feel like a game where learning is intrinsic to its game mechanics.

  2. High-Density Content/Irrelevant Information for Dual Audiences
    Ludera had student engagement features alongside investor-leaning information on a single landing page.
Proposed Solutions
  1. Avoid vocabulary/language that emphasize educational workflows unless absolutely necessary. Instead, frame knowledge acquisition as a gameplay mechanic that unlocks progression, rewards, upgrades, rare loot, cosmetics, etc.

  2. Dedicate 80-90% of the Home Page to: immersion and worldbuilding, lore teasers, game features and visual demonstrations of the game experience. Have a navigation bar (header and/or footer) that instantly routes investors to pages dedicated to data-driven, metric-focused information.
Learning Hurdles
  • Being my own boss (in that we had flexible work hours)
  • Adapting to rapid startup changes
Learning Outcomes
  • UI/UX workflows (ie. user research, user flows, wireframing, prototyping)
  • Learning to make independent design choices for the sake of momentum and deadline adherence, over delayed consensus
